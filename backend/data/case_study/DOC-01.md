Internal summary from the Sales team of deals closed in Q3 2026.

Won: 19 deals. Lost: 7 deals.

Reasons given for lost deals (one main reason per deal):
- Missing integrations: 3
- Price: 2
- Competitor demoed an AI chat assistant: 1
- Chose to build in-house: 1

Sales team comments:
- "Prospects ask 'do you have AI?' in about half of first calls now, but it rarely comes up again once they see the product."
- "The one AI loss was a mid-market prospect. Their evaluation team was impressed by a competitor's chat demo. We don't know if they use it."
- "Nobody has asked what an AI feature would cost. We have never tested pricing for one."

Open question from the Head of Sales: would an AI assistant help us win deals, or is it just something prospects ask about?
