Interview with Dana Okafor, VP of Operations. Northwind is one of our five largest accounts (210 seats).

Dana opened by asking when we will ship "an AI assistant, like ChatGPT inside Tallyboard." She said two other tools her team uses have added one.

When asked what she would use it for, she described her Monday mornings: "I spend about two hours building the weekly ops report for leadership. I open six dashboards, screenshot charts, and write a paragraph explaining why on-time delivery moved. The writing part is what kills me."

Asked what would make the biggest difference: "If I could just ask 'why did on-time delivery drop in the Midwest last week?' and get an answer I can paste into my report."

She said she would not use an assistant for building dashboards. "My analysts already do that fine."

Interviewer note: the request is framed as "AI assistant", but the pain she described is explaining metric changes and assembling the weekly report.
