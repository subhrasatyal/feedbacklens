Interview with Marcus Lee, office manager across four dental clinics (6 seats).

Marcus did not bring up AI. His main frustration is finding things: "We have something like 40 dashboards now. Half of them were made by someone who left. When the owner asks me why cancellations went up, I don't know which dashboard to open."

He usually answers questions by exporting data to a spreadsheet and comparing months by hand. That takes him "most of a Friday afternoon" once a month.

When asked directly about an AI assistant: "If it just told me where to look, sure. I don't need it to write anything. I'd be nervous if it made up a number."

He mentioned he has never clicked on Smart Suggestions. "I thought it was an ad for an upgrade."

Interviewer note: the job is "answer the owner's question quickly." The barrier is finding the right data, not analysis.
