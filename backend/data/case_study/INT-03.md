Interview with Priya Raman, Director of Finance Systems, and Tom Becker from IT security. Crestline is one of our five largest accounts (180 seats).

Priya said Crestline's board has asked every department to "have an AI story" by year end. She would like Tallyboard to have an assistant so she can "show we're using AI in reporting."

When asked which task it would help with, Priya mentioned month-end variance commentary: explaining why actual spend differed from budget for each department. It currently takes her team about three days each month.

Tom raised a concern: "Any AI feature that sends our financial data to a third-party model is a no unless we can turn it off or keep data in our region. Legal would need to review it." He estimated a review would take at least one quarter.

Priya agreed the security review would come first. She could not say whether Crestline would pay extra for an AI feature: "That's a procurement question."

Interviewer note: strong stated demand, partly driven by board pressure. There is a real blocker (data security) and no signal on budget.
