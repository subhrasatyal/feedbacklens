Exit interview with Alicia Gómez, operations lead. Pinecrest cancelled in August 2026 after two years (12 seats).

Alicia said the main reason they left was integrations: "We moved our accounting to QuickBooks and our leasing pipeline to HubSpot. Tallyboard couldn't pull from either without a CSV upload every week. The new tool connects to both."

When asked whether AI features influenced the decision: "Honestly, no. The new tool has some AI thing, but we haven't used it. AI wasn't on our list."

What would have kept them: "If the data just showed up automatically, we'd have stayed. Uploading CSVs every Monday was the problem."

Interviewer note: churn caused by missing integrations. AI was not a factor, even though the competitor offers it.
