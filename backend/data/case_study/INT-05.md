Interview with Sam Whitfield, senior data analyst (75 seats). Sam builds most of Meridian's dashboards.

Sam is skeptical of an AI assistant: "If it gives our CFO a wrong number even once, she will never trust our dashboards again. Then I'm back to building everything in Excel to double-check."

But Sam described a related pain: executives message him several times a week asking "why did this number change?" Each question takes 20 to 40 minutes to investigate and write up.

What would help: "Something that shows the breakdown, like which clinic or which service line drove the change, with the actual numbers. If it explains a change, it has to show its work."

Sam uses Smart Suggestions "occasionally" but finds that it "flags things that don't matter, like a 2% blip on a Sunday."

Interviewer note: there is demand for explaining changes, but trust and traceability are requirements. A generic chat assistant may not meet them.
