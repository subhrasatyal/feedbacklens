Product analytics for Smart Suggestions, our existing feature that highlights unusual changes in a dashboard. It launched March 10, 2026, and is on by default for all accounts.

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Accounts where at least one user clicked a suggestion in the first month | 31% |
| Users who clicked a suggestion in a typical week (September) | 6% |
| Suggestions dismissed without clicking | 78% |
| Most-clicked suggestion type | "Explain this change" breakdown (61% of clicks) |

Notes:
- Weekly use fell from 14% of users in April to 6% in September.
- In an in-app survey (n = 48), the top reason for ignoring suggestions was "not relevant / too many alerts" (52%). The second was "didn't know what it was" (23%).
- Users who clicked "Explain this change" were 2.3 times more likely to return to the feature the following week.
- We have no data on whether customers would pay more for AI or smart features. Smart Suggestions has always been included in all plans.
